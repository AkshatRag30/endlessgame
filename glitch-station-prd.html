1Overview and vision

Glitch Station is a browser first social deduction game for friend groups: share a 4 letter room code, play an 8 to 12 minute match on your phones, and argue about who the Glitch is.

AttributeDecision
GenreOnline social deduction (hidden roles, tasks, meetings, voting)
ViewTop down 2D, portrait on mobile, landscape on desktop
Players5 to 10 per room; 1 Glitch for 5 to 7 players, 2 for 8 to 10
PlatformWeb first, no install; join by link or room code
Match length8 to 12 minutes
AudienceFriend groups, classmates, streamers and Discord communities aged 13 and up
Monetization (later)Cosmetic NFT skins and accessories; no pay to win, no ads in matches

Originality rule: the genre is open, but no names, characters, maps, art, sounds or signature task designs from Among Us or other existing games. Everything in this document is original to Glitch Station.

Win rate balanceCrew wins 45% to 55% of matches

Non goals for v1: voice chat, public matchmaking with strangers, ranked play, more than one map, native apps and real money purchases.

3Core gameplay loop

Each match alternates between a free roam phase, where the crew does tasks and Glitches strike, and meetings, where everyone talks and votes.

  1. Players join a room with a code; the host picks settings and starts.
  2. Roles are revealed privately: Crew or Glitch. Glitches see each other.
  3. Free roam: Crew completes tasks around the station; Glitches fake tasks, sabotage systems and eliminate crew when nobody is watching.
  4. A meeting starts when someone reports a body or presses the emergency button.
  5. Discussion, then voting. The player with the most votes is ejected; ties and skips eject nobody.
  6. Free roam resumes until a win condition is met, then everyone sees the reveal and can rematch with one tap.

4Roles and win conditions

V1 ships with two roles only, so the core deduction is tuned before special roles add complexity.

RoleAbilitiesWins when
CrewDo tasks, fix sabotage, report bodies, call one emergency meeting, voteAll tasks are complete, or every Glitch is ejected
GlitchEliminate (cooldown), travel through conduits, sabotage systems, fake tasks, voteGlitches equal or outnumber the crew, or a critical sabotage timer runs out
Ghost (after elimination)Crew ghosts keep doing tasks; Glitch ghosts can still sabotage; ghosts chat only with ghostsTheir living team wins

Later roles (v2, behind a host toggle): Tracer (sees footprints for 5 seconds), Mender (one shield per round on another player), Jammer (a Glitch who can hide a body for one meeting).

5Map, movement and vision

One hand built map, the Neon Relay, with 9 rooms connected by corridors, sized so a full lap takes about 40 seconds at walking speed.

RoomPurpose
HubSpawn point and emergency button
Core ReactorMeltdown sabotage fix point (needs two players)
Signal TowerSignal alignment task, comms sabotage fix
Server VaultData tasks; the only room with cameras later
Power GridBlackout fix point
Cooling BayFan and coolant tasks
DockCargo tasks, long dead end (risky)
LabSample tasks
LoungeQuiet room, few tasks, good alibi spot

Movement: virtual joystick on mobile (left thumb anywhere), WASD or arrows on desktop. Walking speed 4.5 tiles per second. Use, report and eliminate buttons sit on the right side.

Vision: each player sees a circle of light around them; walls block sight. Crew radius is 6 tiles, Glitch radius 8. During a blackout crew vision drops to 2 tiles. The server only sends positions of players inside a client’s vision, so nobody can cheat by reading network data.

Conduits: 6 conduit openings linked in 3 pairs. Only Glitches can use them; entering and leaving plays a short visible animation, so a sharp crewmate can catch it.

6Tasks

Tasks are 3 to 10 second touch friendly minigames; each crew member gets 2 common, 3 short and 1 long task, and a shared task bar fills as the whole crew progresses.

TaskRoomTypeWhat the player does
Reroute CircuitPower GridShortDrag 4 glowing wires to matching ports
Align SignalSignal TowerShortRotate a dial until two waveforms overlap
Defrag BlocksServer VaultShortSlide coloured blocks into sorted columns
Purge CoolantCooling BayShortHold a valve until the gauge hits the green band
Sort PacketsDockCommonFlick incoming packets left or right by colour
Patch FirewallHubCommonTap breaches in a grid before they spread
Calibrate CoreCore ReactorShortStop three spinning rings at the right moment
Upload BackupLab, then Server VaultLongStart a download in one room, finish the upload in another (two stages, 8 seconds each)

Some tasks show a visible effect on the map (a light turning green), so crew can prove innocence by being seen doing them. Glitches see a fake task list and can stand at tasks, but their task bar never moves.

7Eliminations and sabotage

Glitches win by picking moments carefully: eliminations have a cooldown and a short range, and sabotage splits the crew or forces them into rooms.

ActionRule
EliminateRange 1.2 tiles, cooldown 25 seconds (host setting 15 to 45), leaves a body that stays until a meeting
BlackoutCrew vision drops to 2 tiles until someone flips 5 switches in Power Grid
Core MeltdownCritical: two players must hold the two panels in Core Reactor at the same time within 40 seconds, or Glitches win
Jam CommsTask lists and the task bar are hidden until someone realigns the dish in Signal Tower
Lock DoorsCloses one room’s doors for 10 seconds

Only one sabotage runs at a time, with a 30 second shared cooldown. Emergency meetings cannot be called during a critical sabotage.

8Meetings, chat and voting

Meetings are where the game is won, so chat has to be fast on a phone and safe for a 13 and up audience.

ElementRule
TriggersReport a body, or press the Hub button (1 per player per match, 20 second cooldown after a meeting)
Discussion45 seconds (host setting), voting locked
Voting60 seconds; vote a player or skip; votes reveal at the end
ResultMost votes is ejected; ties or majority skip ejects nobody; host decides whether the ejected role is revealed
Quick chatTap built sentences: “I was in [room]”, “I saw [player] at [room]”, “[player] is sus”, “Skip”, “I did [task]”
Free text chatPrivate rooms only, 80 characters per message, profanity filter, mute and report on every player
VoiceNot in v1; friend groups already use Discord or a call

10Networking architecture

Every room is one authoritative server object that owns the truth (positions, roles, cooldowns, votes); clients only send inputs and draw what the server tells them.

PieceChoice
Room serverOne Cloudflare Durable Object per room, holding all match state in memory, with WebSockets to each player
Tick rateServer simulates at 20 ticks per second and sends each client a snapshot of what that client can see
Client movementClient prediction for your own character, interpolation (100 ms behind) for others, server correction if they drift
MessagesSmall JSON (MessagePack later): input, use, eliminate, report, sabotage, chat, vote; server events: snapshot, role, meeting, result
Room codes4 letters from an alphabet without lookalike letters; the code maps to the Durable Object by name
ReconnectSession token in local storage; rejoin within 60 seconds restores the player
PersistenceNone needed for v1; match results and profiles come later

Cloudflare made Durable Objects available on the Workers Free plan in 2025, built for real time apps like chat and multiplayer games, which keeps the whole backend free during testing. Alternative if Cloudflare does not fit: a self hosted Colyseus server.

11Anti cheat and player safety

In a hidden role game, one leaked role ruins the match, so the server never sends a client anything it should not know.

ThreatProtection
Reading roles from network dataEach client only receives its own role; Glitch teammates are sent only to Glitches
Seeing through wallsServer culls positions outside each player’s vision before sending
Fake eliminations or speed hacksServer checks range, cooldown, role and maximum movement speed on every input
Spam and floodingRate limits per connection on chat, inputs and room creation
Abusive chatProfanity filter, mute, report, host kick, quick chat only in any future public lobby
Young playersNo personal data collected in v1 (nickname only), no direct messages, no free text with strangers

13UI, art and audio

Same neon synthwave family as Lane Siege so the two games feel like one world, but with a readable, high contrast top down view.

ScreenContents
HomeCreate room, join with code, nickname, colour picker
LobbyPlayer list with colours, room code and share button, host settings, ready state
Role revealFull screen card: Crew in cyan, Glitch in red with teammates shown
Free roam HUDTask bar at top, task list (collapsible), mini map button, joystick bottom left, Use / Report / Eliminate / Sabotage buttons bottom right
MeetingGrid of player cards with vote buttons, timer, chat panel, quick chat sheet

Characters are simple rounded suits with a visor and one accessory slot, in 12 colours chosen to stay distinguishable for colour blind players (each colour also has a pattern). Audio: ambient station hum, distinct sounds for task done, body report, meeting start, eject and win.

14Technical requirements

A TypeScript monorepo with a Phaser client and a Cloudflare Workers server, sharing one package of message types and game rules.

AreaChoice
ClientVite, TypeScript, Phaser 3 for rendering, input and tilemaps; HTML overlay for menus and chat
MapBuilt in Tiled (free) and exported as JSON with collision and room layers
ServerCloudflare Workers with one Durable Object class per room, WebSocket Hibernation API
Shared codepackages/shared: message types, constants, validation and pure game rules used by both sides
HostingCloudflare Pages for the client (free tier allows commercial use)
TestingVitest for rules; a headless bot script that fills a room with 9 fake players to load test
Performance60 fps on a 2021 midrange phone; under 2 KB per snapshot per player
AnalyticsEvents for room created, match started, match finished, disconnects, winner

16Agent implementation plan

Give the agent this file and run one phase per session; every phase ends with something you can play with friends on real phones.

Rules to paste at the start of every session

You are building Glitch Station from the attached PRD. Work on ONE phase at a time and stop when its "Done when" checks pass.
Use TypeScript strictly in a monorepo: apps/client (Vite + Phaser 3), apps/server (Cloudflare Workers + Durable Objects), packages/shared (types, constants, rules).
The server is authoritative: clients send inputs only. Never send a client another player's role or any position outside that client's vision.
Keep every tunable number (speeds, cooldowns, timers, vision) in packages/shared/config.ts.
Do not use names, art, sounds or task designs from Among Us or any other existing game.
Do not add any paid service, paid plan or paid API. Use only free tiers unless I say otherwise.
Never commit secrets; use environment variables.
After each phase: run lint and tests, update README with how to run locally and deploy, and list anything you could not finish.

The spend gate

Spend money only after the Phase 7 soft launch shows at least 50 different rooms playing over two weeks with 4 or more matches per room session. If not, fix the game on the free stack first. Your AI agent subscription is outside this plan, and free tier limits change, so check each provider’s pricing page before relying on it. Checked in November 2026.

18Risks and open questions

The biggest risk is not code but people: the game needs 5 or more players at once, so it must win friend groups before it can support strangers.

RiskMitigation
Not enough players in a roomPrivate rooms first, easy sharing by link, practice bots later, target Discord communities and streamers
Looks or plays like a cloneOriginal theme, names, map, tasks and art; review against the originality rule before each release
Lag on mobile networksPrediction and interpolation from day one; test on real 4G every phase
Toxic chatQuick chat by default, filtered free text in private rooms only, mute and report
Free tier limits on a viral dayLimits fail safely instead of billing; upgrade only once the spend gate is passed
